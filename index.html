⚠️ Good to know

  • Privacy: your videos never leave your computer (no server, no upload). The FFmpeg engine runs in WebAssembly inside this tab.
  • First conversion: the engine (~31 MB) downloads once, then stays cached.
  • Speed: expect roughly ⅓ to 1× the video duration depending on your machine (the player only shows 220×176, so conversion stays fast). Keep the tab visible.
  • Sizes: source files up to ~1.5 GB. Beyond that, split them first (the "max duration" setting).
  • AV1: AV1 decoding is not built in (rare for personal use). H.264, H.265/HEVC, VP8/VP9, MPEG-2… are supported.
  • Tuned for the YUNO 300: XviD 800 kbps (all keyframes), guaranteed MP2 audio, picture corrected for the screen — recipe validated on the device.
